Start your first investigation

  1. Open Workstation on iOS, or the operation list on Android, and choose Ghost Session.
  2. Read the briefing and Case Files. Use events or Event Viewer to inspect the timeline.
  3. Preserve a snapshot, choose the response actions, and verify the result.
  4. Use help to see supported commands, or use the buttons in Response Console.

Common questions

Why does a command not work?

The terminal supports a small set of training commands for the current operation. Enter help to see them. It cannot run ordinary shell programs or contact real computers. Some actions require earlier objectives to be completed; read the response message for the next step.

How do I unlock the next case?

Complete the current case’s objectives, including verification. Each Workstation operation starts at 300 XP. A new hint costs 15 XP, and attempting a response before its prerequisites costs 20 XP. Only your best completed score for each case counts toward your rank.

Why did my unfinished attempt restart?

Completed Workstation scores persist locally. An unfinished attempt is not a saved campaign checkpoint and can restart after leaving the operation or closing the app. Opening Help & privacy keeps your active attempt in place.

Does progress sync between platforms?

No. iOS, Android, and browser progress are separate. The iOS story Operations campaign and its Workstation labs also have separate progress tracks.

How can I reset or delete my progress?
  • iOS: return to the Workstation operation list, open Help & privacy, and choose Reset Workstation progress. Confirm the reset. This keeps the separate story Operations progress. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app keeps app data. Manage any device backups separately.
  • Android: open Help & privacy and choose Reset progress, then confirm. Clearing the app’s storage in system settings or uninstalling also removes saved scores. App backup is disabled.
  • Browser review edition: clear that site’s browser data to remove its saved scores. This support website does not store game progress.
Can I play without typing?

Yes. Event Viewer, Case Files, and Response Console provide visual controls for the same Workstation objectives. Text follows system sizing in the native apps. Tell us your device and preferred accessibility tools if a control is difficult to use.

Where are the App Store and Google Play links?

The apps are in development and are not yet available for public download. Verified store links will be added to the home page after launch.

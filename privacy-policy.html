Simple Cybersecurity Lab / Privacy

Your training.
Your device.

Effective October 11, 2026

This policy describes Simple Cybersecurity Lab, developed by LIPAI WANG, and this public support website. It also applies to early native review builds using the working name CyberOps Academy.

The native iOS and Android apps

The current apps work offline. They have no account registration, advertising, analytics SDKs, or app-operated server. They do not send your training commands, scores, or progress to the developer. The apps do not track you across other apps or websites.

The terminal is a simulation. Commands operate only on bundled fictional scenarios. They do not execute a real shell, scan networks, access external targets, or read personal files on your device.

Information stored on your device

The apps save completed scores and progress in local app storage so your unlocks and best results remain available. The iOS story Operations campaign and Workstation labs use separate progress tracks. Training input and an active Workstation attempt are temporary and are not a durable saved checkpoint.

Progress does not synchronize between devices, platforms, or the browser review edition. iOS device backups may include app data under your device’s backup settings. Android app backup is disabled in the current implementation.

Resetting and removing data

  • On iOS, open Help & privacy from the Workstation operation list and confirm Reset Workstation progress. This clears Workstation scores and unlocks while keeping story Operations progress. Deleting the app removes local app data; offloading it does not. Any device backups must be managed separately through your device or backup provider.
  • On Android, confirm Reset progress in Help & privacy. Clearing app storage in system settings or uninstalling also removes saved scores.
  • There is no app account to delete, and the developer has no server copy of your training progress.

This public website

This website contains product information, support instructions, and this policy. It does not run the training game, use analytics scripts, set app cookies, store training progress, or include a contact form. Its pages, styles, and source are public.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and secure the site. That processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an external link or using email is subject to the destination service’s practices.

If you contact support

Email is optional. If you write to the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments are processed to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain relevant support records. Email is handled through the developer’s email provider. Please send only the information needed to explain the issue.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing the address below. Training data held only on your device must be removed using the controls described above.

Children and personal information

The native apps do not request personal information or include social accounts, public profiles, or user-to-user messaging. If a child has sent personal information through support email, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request its removal.

Platform services

Apple, Google, and your device or backup provider may process information under their own policies, including store, device, backup, or diagnostic information you choose to share with them. Those services are separate from the apps’ local training storage.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the apps’ data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Review the policy associated with the version you use.

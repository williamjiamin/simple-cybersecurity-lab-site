Local progress / Clear boundaries

A space to learn.

The native apps work offline with no account, advertising, or analytics. Scores stay on your device. Commands act only inside the fictional training scenario.

These labs simplify incident response for learning. They do not execute a real shell, scan a network, or replace your organization’s procedures.
